# God Project

This story is the plan for NaNoWriMo 2026. *God Project* is a story about a two
people who are abducted by aliens and given technology that makes them
effectively omnipotent. They return to Earth, and need to wrangle their new
lives with godlike powers. The story covers themes of faith, power and
corruption, perception, and identity.
